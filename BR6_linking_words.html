Recommendations

München Tourismus recommends taking several steps. First, the City of Munich should try to boost the number of incoming tourists from France and Spain, e.g. by launching culture-specific marketing campaigns or by initiating collaborations with tour operators. The city could also convince network and low-cost carriers to launch new routes between Munich Airport and major cities in France and Spain. Second, the City of Munich should do more to attract tourists aged 55 and above, for instance by designing customized tour packages for senior travelers. Additionally, the city could reduce the crowds at major tourist attractions during peak season by incentivizing tourists to visit Munich during the low or shoulder season.
